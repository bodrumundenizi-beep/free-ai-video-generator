### System requirements

**Minimum**
- **OS:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- **CPU:** 4 cores with AVX2 (Intel 4th gen / AMD Ryzen or newer)
- **RAM:** 16 GB
- **GPU:** Not required
- **Disk:** About 8 GB free (the app plus the 2.9 GB of AI models; the AI pictures model adds another 4.2 GB)

**Recommended**
- **OS:** Windows 11 (64-bit)
- **CPU:** 6+ cores
- **RAM:** 16 GB
- **GPU:** Optional. A dedicated card with about 8 GB of video memory is fastest. Integrated graphics also work for AI pictures, at about 90 seconds a picture.
